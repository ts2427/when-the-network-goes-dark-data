# External validation: the FCC's 2017 hurricane season report

Generated by `src/15_external_validation.py` from `manifest/external_fcc_2017_report.csv` (v3.1.0). No data were changed because of this check.

**Source.** FCC Public Safety and Homeland Security Bureau (2018). 2017 Atlantic Hurricane Season Impact on Communications: Report and Recommendations (DOC-353805A1). https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-353805A1.pdf. Every county or state figure it gives for Harvey and Irma is transcribed with its page and compared with the matching report: the panel's rows for the study states, and the parsed report rows for Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands, which are outside the study states and not in the panel. "Printed in the report" is the figure the DIRS report itself prints, where it prints one.

**Result.** 80 figures: 70 can be compared with the reports and 10 cannot (switching centers, PSAPs, broadcast stations and providers' filings, which the panel does not hold). Of the 70, 55 match, 4 match once the FCC's dropped decimals are allowed for, 1 is consistent with a figure stated only in words, and 10 differ. Each difference is explained below or marked as not explained. In each, the season report departs from what the DIRS report prints or from the total its rows reconcile to, so none points to an error in the panel.
