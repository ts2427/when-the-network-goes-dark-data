# Errata

Every change to a number in a published release, by the version that made it: what changed, before and after, and why. Generated by `code/src/14_data_repo.py` from the CORRECTION entries of `docs/decisions.md` that changed published data (`code/manifest/errata.csv` transcribes each before and after value, and the build checks that it appears in the entry, word for word).

Process corrections are not listed here: the 15 CORRECTION entries that changed how the analysis was done before its numbers were published, or only the wording of a draft, are in `docs/decisions.md` only. Chapter keys (`chapter_3` and so on) are those of `results.json`; the page numbers its chapters differently (README, Page chapters and results.json keys). To report an error: `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
